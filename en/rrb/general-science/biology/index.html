General Science: Biology

General Science: Biology

Cell Biology & Genetics

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

Cell: Basic unit of life. Discovered by Robert Hooke (1665). Cell Theory: Schleiden & Schwann. Mitochondria: Powerhouse of cell (ATP). Ribosome: Protein Factory. Lysosome: Suicide Bag. Nucleus: Control Center (contains DNA). Genetics: Father - Gregor Mendel (Pea plants). DNA Double Helix model by Watson & Crick.

Deep Dive

Cell Structure

  • Prokaryotic: No defined nucleus (Bacteria/Blue-green algae).
  • Eukaryotic: Defined nucleus (Plants/Animals).
  • Plant vs Animal Cell: Plant cells have Cell Wall (Cellulose) and Chloroplasts (Photosynthesis); Animal cells do not.

Important Cell Organelles

  • Golgi Bodies: Packaging and secretion.
  • Endoplasmic Reticulum: Transport system.
  • Vacuoles: Storage sacs (Large in plants).

Cell Division

  • Mitosis: Somatic cells (Growth/Repair). 2 daughter cells.
  • Meiosis: Reproductive cells (Gametes). 4 daughter cells (Half chromosomes).

Human Physiology - I

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

Digestive System: Mouth (Starch) -> Stomach (Protein) -> Small Intestine (Complete digestion/Absorption). Liver secretes Bile. Blood: pH 7.4. RBC (O2 transport), WBC (Immunity), Platelets (Clotting). Universal Donor: O-, Recipient: AB+. Circulatory System: Heart (4 chambers). Artery (Pure blood), Vein (Impure blood). Pulmonary artery carries impure blood.

Deep Dive

Digestive Enzymes

  • Saliva: Ptyalin (Amylase).
  • Stomach: Pepsin, Renin (Milk).
  • Pancreas: Trypsin, Lipase, Amylase.
  • Small Intestine: Maltase, Sucrase, Lactase.

Heart & Blood

  • Blood Pressure: 120/80 mmHg (Sphygmomanometer).
  • William Harvey discovered Blood Circulation.
  • Karl Landsteiner discovered Blood Groups.

Human Physiology - II

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

Respiratory System: Lungs. Functional unit: Alveoli. Aerobic (with O2) vs Anaerobic (without O2). Excretory System: Kidney filters blood. Unit: Nephron. Waste: Urea. Nervous System: Brain (Cerebrum - thinking, Cerebellum - balance). Unit: Neuron. Endocrine System: Ductless glands. Pituitary (Master gland), Thyroid (Goitre), Pancreas (Insulin).

Deep Dive

Bones & Muscles

  • Total Bones: 206 (Adult). Muscles: ~639.
  • Femur: Longest bone (Thigh).
  • Stapes: Smallest bone (Ear).
  • Enamel: Hardest substance (Teeth).

Glands & Hormones

  • Adrenal: Emergency hormone (Adrenaline).
  • Pancreas: Mixed gland (Exocrine + Endocrine). Secretes Insulin (Controls blood sugar).
  • Thyroid: Thyroxine (Controls metabolism). Needed Iodine.

Diseases & Nutrition

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

Vitamins:

  • A (Retinol): Night Blindness.
  • B1 (Thiamine): Beri-Beri.
  • C (Ascorbic Acid): Scurvy.
  • D (Calciferol): Rickets.
  • K (Phylloquinone): Non-clotting of blood. Diseases:
  • Virus: AIDS, Polio, Dengue, Rabies, Small Pox.
  • Bacteria: TB, Typhoid, Cholera, Tetanus.
  • Protozoa: Malaria, Kala-azar.
Deep Dive

Vaccines

  • Edward Jenner: Small Pox (First vaccine).
  • Louis Pasteur: Rabies.
  • BCG: Tuberculosis.
  • Salk/Sabin: Polio.
  • DPT: Diphtheria, Pertussis, Tetanus.

Botany & Ecology

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

Photosynthesis: CO2 + H2O -> Glucose + O2. Occurs in Chloroplasts (Chlorophyll) during day. Plant Hormones: Auxin (Growth), Gibberellin (Stem height), Ethylene (Fruit ripening). Tissues: Xylem (Water transport), Phloem (Food transport). Ecology: Study of interactions. Ecosystem.

Deep Dive

Classification

  • Five Kingdoms: Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, Animalia (R.H. Whittaker).
  • Study of:
    • Mycology: Fungi.
    • Phycology: Algae.
    • Pomology: Fruits.
    • Entomology: Insects.
