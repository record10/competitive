General Science: Chemistry

General Science: Chemistry

Matter & Atomic Structure

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

Matter: Anything that has mass and occupies space. States: Solid, Liquid, Gas, Plasma, BEC. Atom: Smallest unit. Dalton's Atomic Theory. Subatomic Particles: Proton (Rutherford/Goldstein, +ve), Electron (J.J. Thomson, -ve), Neutron (Chadwick, neutral). Atomic Number (Z): No. of protons. Mass Number (A): Protons + Neutrons.

Deep Dive

Isotopes, Isobars, Isotones

  • Isotopes: Same Atomic No, Different Mass No (e.g., C-12, C-14). (Hydrogen: Protium, Deuterium, Tritium).
  • Isobars: Different Atomic No, Same Mass No (e.g., Argon, Calcium).
  • Isotones: Same number of neutrons.

Models

  • Thomson: Plum Pudding Model.
  • Rutherford: Gold Foil Experiment (Discovered Nucleus).
  • Bohr: Energy shells (K, L, M, N).

Acids, Bases & Salts

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

Acids: Sour taste, Blue litmus to Red. pH < 7. (e.g., HCl, H2SO4, Citric Acid). Bases: Bitter taste, Red litmus to Blue. pH > 7. (e.g., NaOH, KOH). Salts: Acid + Base -> Salt + Water (Neutralization). pH = 7 (Neutral). pH Scale: Sorensen (0 to 14). Blood pH 7.4.

Deep Dive

Important Acids

  • HCl: Gastric juice (Digestion).
  • H2SO4: King of Chemicals (Car batteries).
  • HNO3: Aqua Regia (with HCl in 1:3) dissolves Gold.
  • Acetic Acid: Vinegar.
  • Formic Acid: Ant sting.

Common Salts

  • NaCl: Common Salt.
  • Na2CO3: Washing Soda.
  • NaHCO3: Baking Soda.
  • CaOCl2: Bleaching Powder.

Periodic Table & Elements

Development: Dobereiner (Triads), Newlands (Octaves), Mendeleev (Atomic Mass). Modern Periodic Table: Henry Moseley (Atomic Number). Structure: 7 Periods (Rows), 18 Groups (Columns). Metals: Left side (Loss e-). Non-Metals: Right side (Gain e-). Metalloids: Staircase (Si, Ge).

Deep Dive

Groups

  • Group 1: Alkali Metals (Li, Na, K).
  • Group 2: Alkaline Earth Metals (Mg, Ca).
  • Group 17: Halogens (F, Cl, Br, I).
  • Group 18: Noble/Inert Gases (He, Ne, Ar - Zero valency).

Key Element Facts

  • Lightest Element: Hydrogen.
  • Heaviest (Natural): Uranium.
  • Lightest Metal: Lithium.
  • Heaviest Metal: Osmium.
  • Liquid at Room Temp: Mercury (Metal), Bromine (Non-Metal).

Carbon & Its Compounds

Allotropes: Diamond (Hardest natural substance), Graphite (Good conductor), Fullerenes. Hydrocarbons: Alkanes (Single bond, Saturated), Alkenes (Double), Alkynes (Triple). CNG: Compressed Natural Gas (Methane CH4). LPG: Liquefied Petroleum Gas (Butane + Propane).

Deep Dive

Combustion & Flame

  • Fuel: Solid (Coal), Liquid (Petrol), Gas (CNG).
  • Coal Types: Anthracite (Best, 90% C), Bituminous, Lignite, Peat (Worst).
  • Fire Extinguishers: Use CO2 or Baking Soda.

Everyday Chemistry

Alloys: Mixture of metals.

  • Brass: Cu + Zn.
  • Bronze: Cu + Sn.
  • Stainless Steel: Fe + Cr + Ni.
