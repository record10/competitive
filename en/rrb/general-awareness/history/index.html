Modern Indian History

Revolt of 1857 (First War of Independence)

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

Immediate Cause: Introduction of Enfield Rifle (greased cartridges with beef/pork fat). Start Date: May 10, 1857 (Meerut). First Martyr: Mangal Pandey (Barrackpore). Symbol: Lotus and Bread (Chappati). Result: End of East India Company rule; Crown Rule began (Govt of India Act 1858).

Deep Dive

The Revolt of 1857 was the first major expression of Indian resistance against the British East India Company.

Causes:

  • Political: Doctrine of Lapse (Dalhousie) - annexation of Satara, Jhansi, etc.
  • Economic: Heavy taxation, destruction of handicrafts.
  • Military: Discrimination in pay/rank; General Service Enlistment Act.
  • Social: Interference in customs (Sati abolition, Widow remarriage).

Key Leaders: Bahadur Shah II (Delhi), Nana Saheb & Tantia Tope (Kanpur), Begum Hazrat Mahal (Lucknow), Rani Laxmibai (Jhansi), Kunwar Singh (Bihar).

Reasons for failure: Lack of central leadership, no common ideology, limited territorial spread (South India unaffected), superior British military strength.

Impact: End of Company Rule (Govt of India Act 1858). Viceroy title introduced (Canning).

Socio-Religious Reforms

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

Brahmo Samaj (1828): Raja Ram Mohan Roy - Abolition of Sati. Arya Samaj (1875): Swami Dayanand Saraswati - "Go back to Vedas". Ramakrishna Mission (1897): Swami Vivekananda - Humanitarianism. Satyashodhak Samaj (1873): Jyotiba Phule - Anti-caste movement. Prarthana Samaj (1867): Atmaram Pandurang - Widow remarriage.

Deep Dive

Raja Ram Mohan Roy: "Father of Indian Renaissance". Established Brahmo Samaj (1828). Efforts led to anti-Sati Regulation (1829). Published Mirat-ul-Akhbar.

Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar: Championed widow remarriage (Act passed in 1856).

Swami Dayanand Saraswati: Founded Arya Samaj (1875). Slogan: "Go back to Vedas". Wrote Satyarth Prakash. Started Shuddhi Movement.

Swami Vivekananda: Disciple of Ramakrishna. Famous Chicago Speech (1893). Founded Ramakrishna Mission (1897). "Service to Jiva is service to Shiva".

Jyotiba Phule: Founded Satyashodhak Samaj (1873) in Maharashtra. Wrote Gulamgiri. Fought against caste oppression.

Indian National Congress (Est. 1885)

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

Founded: in 1885 by A.O. Hume. First Session: Bombay, W.C. Banerjee (President). 1907 Surat Split: Congress split into Moderates and Extremists. 1916 Lucknow Pact: Reunion of Moderates and Extremists, Congress-League unity. 1929 Lahore Session: Purna Swaraj Resolution under J.L. Nehru.

Deep Dive

Moderate Phase (1885-1905): Leaders: Dadabhai Naoroji, G.K. Gokhale. Believed in "3Ps" (Petition, Prayer, Protest). Demanded dominion status.

Extremist Phase (1905-1919): Triggered by Bengal Partition (1905). Leaders: Lal-Bal-Pal (Lala Lajpat Rai, Tilak, Bipin Chandra Pal) & Aurobindo Ghosh. Demanded 'Swaraj'. Tilak: "Swaraj is my birthright".

Gandhian Phase (1919-1947): Mass struggle based on Truth (Satya) and Non-violence (Ahimsa).

Important Sessions:

  • Surat (1907): Split (Moderates vs Extremists).
  • Lucknow (1916): Reunion (Lucknow Pact with Muslim League).
  • Lahore (1929): Purna Swaraj resolution (Nehru).
  • Karachi (1931): Fundamental Rights (Patel).

Freedom Struggle Timeline (1905-1947)

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

1905: Partition of Bengal, Swadeshi Movement. 1919: Jallianwala Bagh Massacre, Rowlatt Act. 1920: Non-Cooperation Movement. 1930: Civil Disobedience Movement, Dandi March. 1942: Quit India Movement ("Do or Die"). 1947: Indian Independence Act.

Deep Dive

1905-1915 (Extremist Phase):

  • 1905: Partition of Bengal. Swadeshi Movement.
  • 1906: Muslim League founded.
  • 1913: Ghadar Party (San Francisco).

1916-1930 (Gandhian Era Begins):

  • 1917: Champaran Satyagraha (First Civil Disobedience).
  • 1919: Jallianwala Bagh Massacre (Apr 13). Rowlatt Act.
  • 1920: Non-Cooperation Movement.
  • 1922: Chauri Chaura incident (NCM withdrawn).
  • 1930: Dandi March (Civil Disobedience).

1931-1947:

  • 1931: Gandhi-Irwin Pact.
  • 1942: Quit India Movement ("Do or Die").
  • 1947: Independence.

Revolutionary Activities: HRA/HSRA (Kakori 1925, Bhagat Singh). INA (Subhash Chandra Bose - "Jai Hind", "Dilli Chalo").

Key Governor Generals & Viceroys

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

Warren Hastings (1773-85): First Governor General of Bengal. Lord Cornwallis (1786-93): Permanent Settlement, Father of Civil Services. Lord Wellesley (1798-1805): Subsidiary Alliance. Lord William Bentinck (1828-35): Abolition of Sati, English Education. Lord Dalhousie (1848-56): Doctrine of Lapse, Railways, Telegraph. Lord Canning (1856-62): Revolt of 1857, First Viceroy. Lord Mountbatten (1947): Last Viceroy, First GG of Free India.

Deep Dive

Warren Hastings (1773-85): First GG of Bengal. Regulating Act 1773.

Cornwallis (1786-93): Permanent Settlement, Civil Services.

Wellesley (1798-1805): Subsidiary Alliance (Hyderabad first to accept).

William Bentinck (1828-35): Abolition of Sati (1829). First GG of India. English Education (Macaulay).

Dalhousie (1848-56): Doctrine of Lapse (Satara, Jhansi). Railways (1853), Telegraph.

Canning (1856-62): Revolt of 1857. First Viceroy.

Ripon (1880-84): Father of Local Self Govt. Repealed Vernacular Press Act.

Curzon (1899-1905): Partition of Bengal.

Mountbatten (1947): Last Viceroy. Plan for Partition (June 3 Plan).
