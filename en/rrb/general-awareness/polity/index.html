Indian Polity & Constitution

Indian Polity & Constitution

Indian Constitution: Making & Features

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

Constituent Assembly: Formed 1946 (Cabinet Mission). President: Dr. Rajendra Prasad. Drafting Committee: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar. Adoption: 26 Nov 1949. Enactment: 26 Jan 1950. Sources: USA (Fundamental Rights), UK (Parliamentary System), Ireland (DPSP), Australia (Concurrent List), Germany (Emergency). Schedules: Originally 8, Currently 12.

Deep Dive

1. Constituent Assembly

  • Formation: 1946 under Cabinet Mission Plan.
  • First Meeting: 9 Dec 1946 (Interim President: Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha).
  • Permanent President: Dr. Rajendra Prasad (Elected 11 Dec 1946).
  • Constitutional Advisor: B.N. Rau.
  • Drafting Committee: Chairman Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (Formed 29 Aug 1947).
  • Time Taken: 2 years, 11 months, 18 days.

2. Major Sources

  • UK: Parliamentary rule, Single citizenship, Rule of Law, Writs.
  • USA: Fundamental Rights, Judicial Review, Impeachment of President, Post of VP.
  • Ireland: DPSP, Method of President Election.
  • Australia: Concurrent List, Joint Sitting of Parliament.
  • Canada: Federal structure (Strong Centre), Residuary powers.
  • USSR (Russia): Fundamental Duties.
  • Germany (Weimar): Suspension of Fundamental Rights during Emergency.
  • South Africa: Amendment procedure.

3. Schedules

  • Sch 1: States & UTs.
  • Sch 2: Salary & Allowances.
  • Sch 3: Oaths & Affirmations.
  • Sch 4: Rajya Sabha Seat Allocation.
  • Sch 5: Admin of SC/ST areas.
  • Sch 6: Tribal areas of Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram (AMTM).
  • Sch 7: Division of Powers (Union List-100, State List-61, Concurrent List-52).
  • Sch 8: Languages (Orig 14, Now 22).
  • Sch 9: Land Reforms (1st Amd 1951).
  • Sch 10: Anti-Defection Law (52nd Amd 1985).
  • Sch 11: Panchayats (29 subjects) - 73rd Amd.
  • Sch 12: Municipalities (18 subjects) - 74th Amd.

Fundamental Rights & Duties

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

Part III (Art 12-35): Magna Carta of India. 6 Fundamental Rights: Equality (14-18), Freedom (19-22), Against Exploitation (23-24), Religion (25-28), Culture & Education (29-30), Constitutional Remedies (32). Fundamental Duties (Part IV-A, Art 51A): Swaran Singh Committee, 42nd Amd (1976). Total 11.

Deep Dive

Fundamental Rights (Part III)

1. Right to Equality (14-18)

  • Art 14: Equality before law.
  • Art 15: Prohibition of discrimination on grounds of religion, race, caste, sex or place of birth.
  • Art 16: Equality of opportunity in public employment.
  • Art 17: Abolition of Untouchability.
  • Art 18: Abolition of titles (except military/academic).

2. Right to Freedom (19-22)

  • Art 19: Freedom of Speech (19(1)(a) includes press).
  • Art 20: Protection in respect of conviction for offenses.
  • Art 21: Protection of Life and Personal Liberty. 21A: Right to Education (6-14 yrs).
  • Art 22: Protection against arrest in certain cases.

3. Right against Exploitation (23-24)

  • Art 23: Prohibition of human trafficking and forced labor.
  • Art 24: Prohibition of child labor in factories (<14 yrs).

4. Right to Freedom of Religion (25-28)

  • Art 25: Freedom of conscience and practice.

5. Cultural & Educational Rights (29-30)

  • Protection of interests of minorities.

6. Right to Constitutional Remedies (32)

  • Art 32: Dr. Ambedkar called it the "Heart and Soul of Constitution". SC can issue 5 Writs.

Fundamental Duties (Part IV-A)

  • Source: USSR.
  • Amendment: 42nd Amd (1976) by Swaran Singh Committee.
  • Count: Originally 10, 11th duty (Education) added by 86th Amd (2002).

Union Executive: President & Parliament

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

President (Art 52): First Citizen. Election (Single Transferable Vote). Impeachment (Art 61). Pardon (Art 72). Parliament (Art 79): President + Rajya Sabha (Art 80) + Lok Sabha (Art 81). Rajya Sabha: Permanent House. Members 250. Lok Sabha: Lower House. Members 550. Speaker powers (Money Bill). Prime Minister (Art 75): Real Head.

Deep Dive

President

  • Art 52: There shall be a President of India.
  • Art 54: Electoral College (Elected members of LS, RS and State Assemblies).
  • Art 61: Impeachment - for violation of Constitution.
  • Art 72: Pardoning Power.
  • Art 123: Power to promulgate Ordinances.

Parliament

  • Structure: President, Rajya Sabha (Upper House), Lok Sabha (Lower House).

Rajya Sabha (Council of States):

  • Max Seats: 250 (Current 245).
  • Nominated: 12 members (Arts, Lit, Science, Social Service).
  • Nature: Permanent House (Never dissolved). Term 6 years.
  • Ex-officio Chairman: Vice President (Art 64).

Lok Sabha (House of the People):

  • Max Seats: 550.
  • Powers: Money Bill (Art 110) introduced ONLY in LS. Speaker presides over Joint Sitting (Art 108).
  • Speaker: Decides if bill is Money Bill. Resigns to Deputy Speaker.

Judiciary & State Government

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

Supreme Court (Art 124): CJI + 33 others. Retirement 65 yrs. High Court (Art 214): For States. Retirement 62 yrs. Governor (Art 153): Constitutional Head of State. Appointed by President. Chief Minister (Art 164): Real Head of State.

Deep Dive

Supreme Court

  • Est: 28 Jan 1950 (Art 124).
  • Judges: 1 CJI + 33 others (Total 34).
  • Appointment: By President via Collegium System.
  • Writ Jurisdiction: Art 32 (Fundamental Rights).

High Court

  • Art 214: High Court for each state.
  • Total HCs: Currently 25. (Newest: Andhra/Telangana).
  • Writ Jurisdiction: Art 226 (Fundamental Rights & others - Wider than SC).

State Executive

  • Governor: Holds office during pleasure of President. Constitutional Head. Pardoning Power (Art 161 - cannot pardon death sentence).
  • Chief Minister (CM): Leader of majority party. Link between Governor and Council of Ministers.

Important Amendments & Bodies

RRB NTPC RRB Group D RRB ALP RRB JE

Important Amd: 42nd (Mini Cost., Preamble words), 44th (Property Right removed), 61st (Voting age 21 to 18), 73rd/74th (Panchayat/Muni), 86th (Education), 101st (GST). Const Bodies: Election Comm (324), UPSC (315), Finance Comm (280), CAG (148).

Deep Dive

Important Constitutional Amendments

  • 42nd (1976): "Mini Constitution". Added 'Socialist, Secular, Integrity' to Preamble. Added Fundamental Duties.
  • 44th (1978): Removed Right to Property from FR to Legal Right (300A).
  • 61st (1989): Reduced voting age from 21 to 18.
  • 73rd & 74th (1992): Panchayati Raj & Municipalities.
  • 86th (2002): Right to Education (21A).
  • 101st (2016): GST.
  • 103rd (2019): 10% EWS Reservation.
  • 106th (2023): 33% Women Reservation in LS/Assemblies.

Constitutional Bodies

  • Election Commission (Art 324): Free & fair elections. 3 member body.
  • CAG (Art 148): Comptroller & Auditor General. Guardian of public purse.
  • Finance Commission (Art 280): Distribution of taxes between Centre & State. (Every 5 yrs by President).
  • UPSC (Art 315): Recruitment for All India Services.
  • Attorney General (Art 76): Highest Law Officer. Can attend Parliament but cannot vote.
