Seating Arrangement

IBPS PO Pre SBI Clerk Pre

Linear Arrangement Rules:

  • North Facing: Left = Your Left, Right = Your Right.

  • South Facing: Left = Your Right, Right = Your Left.

  • Parallel Rows: Row 1 (South) faces Row 2 (North).

Circular/Square Rules:

  • Facing Center: Left = Clockwise, Right = Anti-clockwise.

  • Facing Outside: Left = Anti-clockwise, Right = Clockwise.

  • "Who/Which": Refers to the immediate predecessor subject.

  • "And/But": Refers to the first subject of the sentence.

Deep Dive

1. The "Whose" and "And" Confusion Understanding the grammar is 50% of the solution. Statement: "A sits 2nd to the left of B, who is 3rd to right of C." Here, 'who' refers to B. So B is 3rd to right of C. Statement: "A sits 2nd to the left of B and is 3rd to right of C." Here, 'and' refers to A. So A is 3rd to right of C.

2. Thread Method (Open Circle) Instead of drawing a circle, draw a straight line. • Assume everyone faces North. • If A is second to left of B, place A left of B. • If you reach the end of the line, 'wrap around' to the start. Benefit: No confusion about Left/Right turns.

3. Uncertain Number of Persons "A certain number of persons are sitting in a row..." Strategy:

  1. Start with a solid connection like "A sits 3rd to right of B".
  2. Merge individual clues strings.
  3. Count total at the very end.

Floor, Box & Flat Puzzles

IBPS PO Pre SBI PO Pre

Floor Puzzle Basics:

  • Ground floor is number 1, Top floor is number N.

  • Gap Calculation: "3 people live between A and B" means position difference is 4.

  • Even/Odd: Watch out for "A lives on an Odd numbered floor".

Box Puzzle Logic:

  • No fixed positions (unlike floors).

  • "Just Above/Below": Immediate neighbor.

  • "Number of boxes": "Boxes above A is same as below B".

Deep Dive

1. Box Puzzles Strategy These are tricky because there is no base framework like Floor 1-8. Step 1: Don't number 1 to 10 immediately. Step 2: Find the "Anchor" statement that connects at least 3-4 boxes. Step 3: Draw two parallel cases.

2. Flat & Floor Puzzles "4 Floors (1-4) and 2 Flats (P, Q) on each floor." This is essentially a grid/matrix puzzle. Common Trap: "A lives west of B" means A and B are on the SAME floor.

3. Month & Date Based Puzzles "8 people born on two dates (7th, 14th) of 4 months." Tip: Always write the Total Days of the month next to the Month Name. Jan (31), Mar (31), Apr (30), Jun (30).

Scheduling & Advanced Puzzles

IBPS PO Mains SBI Clerk Mains

Scheduling Types:

  • Day Based: Mon to Sun.

  • Date & Month: 2 Dates × 4 Months.

  • Year Based: Born in 1990, 1995, etc. (Calc Age first).

Advanced Concepts:

  • Blood Relation Seating: "A sits right of his wife". Solve Relation Tree first.

  • Triangular Arrangement: 3 at corners, 3 at middles.

Deep Dive

1. Day & Week Puzzles "7 people go to movies from Mon to Sun." Approach: Fix the Days column (Mon-Sun). Don't fix Persons. Keywords: • "A goes before Wednesday" → Mon or Tue. • "Two people go between A and B". (Case 1: A..B, Case 2: B..A).

2. Year Based (Age) Puzzles "Persons born in 1980, 1985, 1992..." Step 1: Write years in descending order (Oldest first). Step 2: Calculate Age relative to "Base Date". Step 3: Solve clues using Age numbers.

3. Concentric Square/Circle Asked in Mains. Two tables, one inside another. • Outer table faces inside. • Inner table faces outside. Tip: Treat this as Parallel Rows arrangement. The curvature doesn't matter, only the "Facing" logic matters.
